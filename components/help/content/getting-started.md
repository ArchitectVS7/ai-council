# Getting Started with AI Council

## What is AI Council?

AI Council is a sophisticated multi-persona collaboration platform that orchestrates expert AI personas to explore creative concepts, strategic challenges, and complex topics from multiple professional perspectives. Think of it as assembling a custom expert panel where each participant brings unique domain expertise - but instead of human experts, you're working with AI personas designed to represent different professional approaches and creative specializations.

## Quick Start Guide

### 1. Your First Discussion (5 minutes)

**Step 1: Enter a Project Brief**
- Navigate to the main interface
- In the project input field, enter a creative brief or strategic challenge like:
  - "I want to create a mobile game about space wizards in cosmic combat with alien gods"
  - "Develop a new social media platform focused on mental health support"
  - "Design a sustainable transportation solution for urban commuters"

**Step 2: Select Workflow Template**
Choose from several pre-configured expert panels:
- **Default Discussion** (3 personas): Empathy Advocate, Moderator, Skeptical Academic
- **Creative Project Development** (5 personas): Creative Visionary, Technical Specialist, Market Analyst, UX Designer, Project Synthesizer
- **Product Strategy Development** (5 personas): Market Strategist, Customer Advocate, Business Analyst, Innovation Catalyst, Implementation Planner
- **Game Development Ideation** (6 personas): Narrative Designer, Genre Specialist, Technical Expert, Game Designer, Market Analyst, Creative Director

**Step 3: Begin the Collaboration**
- Click "Begin Session" to start
- Watch each expert contribute their specialized perspective
- Click "Continue" after each response to advance
- Synthesis personas automatically extract key insights and recommendations

**Step 4: Review Creative Insights**  
- After completion, comprehensive analysis and recommendations are generated
- Review creative concepts, technical feasibility, and market insights
- Export your creative brief, strategic plan, or implementation roadmap

## Key Benefits

- **Multi-perspective Analysis**: Examine topics through different analytical lenses simultaneously
- **Structured Discourse**: Organized, round-based discussions with clear progression
- **Configurable Participants**: Customize AI personas to match your specific needs
- **Professional Documentation**: Generate comprehensive reports in multiple formats
- **Persistent Storage**: Save and revisit discussions with full database integration
- **Easy Sharing**: Export configurations to collaborate with colleagues
